\section{Adversarial Severity Ladders Pipeline} \label{sec:pipeline}

\subsection{Pipeline Overview}
We introduce the Adversarial Severity Ladders (ASL) pipeline, an automated construction approach operating under human oversight: the taxonomy, severity definitions, and escalation rules are authored and reviewed by the human authors, with four advantages over fully human annotation: (1) scale without annotator self-selection bias; (2) programmatic severity calibration via escalation rules, bypassing inter-rater inconsistency on fine-grained severity judgments~\citep{aroyo2015truth}; (3) deterministic reproducibility given fixed seeds and checkpoints; and (4) no large-scale harmful-content exposure for workers, which carries documented psychological risks~\citep{steiger2021psychological}.
The pipeline operates in four stages.

\noindent\textbf{Stage 1: Safe Prompt Generation.}
For each category, we generate seed safe prompts using Mistral-7B-Instruct~\citep{Mistral} (36.5\%), Llama-2-7b-chat-hf~\citep{llama2} (31.6\%), Dolphin-2.9-Llama3-8B~\citep{hartford2024dolphin} (23.4\%), and Vicuna-7B~\citep{vicuna} (8.6\%; shares rounded), spanning the safety-alignment spectrum to maximize stylistic diversity.
Near-duplicate prompts are removed via FAISS clustering on \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2} embeddings (cosine threshold $\geq 0.95$), retaining one representative per cluster.

\noindent\textbf{Stage 2: Risky Prompt Synthesis.}
Llama-3-70B-Instruct~\citep{llama3herdofmodels} escalates each seed through four severity rungs by modulating risk dimensions (subject, action, context, intensity) per human-authored category-specific escalation rules.
Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct~\citep{qwen2.5technicalreport} scores each ladder; any adjacent gap below $0.4$ in the normalized 0--3 risk score triggers regeneration.

\noindent\textbf{Stage 3: Reference Image Generation.}
Images are generated using SDXL~\citep{podell2023sdxl} (55.8\%), Z-Turbo~\citep{zimage2025efficient} (26.8\%), FLUX.1-dev~\citep{labs2025flux1} (16.4\%), and SD3.5-Large~\citep{sd35large} (1.1\%) with safety checkers disabled.
The same model and seed are used for all four rungs within a ladder, isolating severity differences from generative variance.

\noindent\textbf{Stage 4: Verification.}
Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking~\citep{qwen3vl2025} verifies each prompt-image pair for content alignment and severity match (tolerance $\leq 1$ rung) and generates per-image risk explanations; failing pairs are regenerated.
Verification is per-rung and description-guided (unlike Insight~5's unguided filters); cross-rung continuity is audited (\SuppSec{\ref{app:construction:continuity}}).
\red{Incomplete ladders are excluded, yielding 1,026 validated ladders (${\sim}32$ A100 GPU-hours total).}

\subsection{Dataset Quality Analysis} \label{sec:quality}
\Tab{\ref{tab:quality_full}} reports four quality dimensions across all 11 categories.
\textbf{Severity monotonicity.} Spearman $\rho = 0.962$ between rung index and verifier risk scores measures rubric-internal consistency of the construction pipeline (images are verified to within $\pm 1$ rung of their assigned label; Stage~4); human grounding of the rung labels is established separately by the blinded validation study ($\kappa = 0.73$; \SuppSec{\ref{app:human}}).
\textbf{Adversarial stealthiness} (mean stealthy rate 72.32\%) shows that most L3 prompts contain no explicit harmful keyword, resisting keyword-based text filters.
\textbf{Prompt fluency} (mean PPL@L3 $= 33.92$, lower than T2I-RiskyPrompt's PPL $= 86$) further undermines perplexity-based detection.
\textbf{Semantic escalation magnitude} (mean $\Delta\theta = 40.23\degree$) quantifies the angular distance from L0 to L3.
\textbf{Independent relabeling.} Claude-3.7-Sonnet re-labels 100 ladders (400 pairs); the automated evaluator agrees on 96\% (Cohen's $\kappa = 0.76$).
\textbf{Lexical diversity.} Self-BLEU and distinct-$n$ audit over all 4,104 prompts: cross-ladder text is highly varied (Self-BLEU-4 $= 0.05$, distinct-2 $= 0.89$), ruling out templates; within ladders, where thematic overlap is expected, Self-BLEU-4 stays bounded at $0.14$ (distinct-2 $= 0.76$).
