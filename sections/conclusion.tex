\section{Conclusion}
\looseness=-1
\red{\dataname{} shows T2I safety evaluation has been measuring the wrong target: mechanisms certify safety at the explicit extreme while deployment risk lives in the L1--L2 regime.}
\red{Across 6 models, 9 defenses, and 6 filters, attacks exploit this gap structurally, and a ladder-tuned filter closes it to 2.2--2.4\% bypass (matched zero-shot anchors 37.5--65.3\%): the gap is trainable, not intrinsic.}
Closing it requires severity-calibrated response across the spectrum, not L3 suppression alone.
