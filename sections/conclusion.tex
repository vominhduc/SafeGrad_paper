\section{Conclusion}

T2I safety evaluation has been measuring the wrong target: existing mechanisms certify safety at the explicit extreme while deployment risk lives in the L1--L2 regime, where prompts appear benign yet images do not.
\dataname (1,026 ladders $\times$ 4 rungs, 11 categories, with prompts, reference images, and per-rung risk explanations) makes this gap measurable through graded relational evaluation rather than binary labels.
Across 6 models, 9 defenses, and 6 filters, attacks exploit the gap structurally and a ladder-tuned filter closes it to 2.2--2.4\% bypass under prompt-aware inference (matched zero-shot anchors 37.5--65.3\%), including fresh generations from all six models: the gap is trainable, not intrinsic. Closing it requires severity-calibrated response across the full spectrum, not L3 suppression alone; we release \dataname and the ASL pipeline for that effort.
