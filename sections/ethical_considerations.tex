\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\looseness=-1
\dataname{} involves generating harmful content. All data is synthetic and spot-checked for personal information, and human participants (\SuppSec{\ref{app:human}}) were informed and could skip any sample.
We release the following under CC BY-NC 4.0 for research use only: (i) all 1,026 validated ladders with prompt--explanation pairs and per-image verdicts; (ii) the ASL pipeline and evaluation scripts; (iii) L0--L1 reference images openly, with L2--L3 gated to verified researchers; and (iv) the datasheet.
\looseness=-1
The Minors subset is gated further, because the legality of synthetic imagery of minors depends on jurisdiction even for research. We will obtain legal review, replace photorealistic L3 renders with stylized ones where required, and require an explicit use agreement; per-image verdicts and prompts suffice to reproduce the analysis. Affected developers are notified 60 days ahead of release.
