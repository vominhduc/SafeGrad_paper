\section{Discussion}

\noindent\textbf{Calibration targets must move to the boundary.}
All evaluated mechanisms are calibrated on binary distinctions and steepen the severity gradient, so sensitive deployments must evaluate L1--L2 explicitly rather than trust claims derived from L3. The text-to-image handoff compounds this: text filters miss visually adversarial prompts (\Sec{\ref{sec:quality}}), and image classifiers trained on explicit L3 content miss L1--L2 violations.
