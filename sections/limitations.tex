\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with safety checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses are tested only on SD1.4 because weight-modifying methods need architecture-specific reimplementation for DiT backbones (paradigm rankings may not transfer).

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} Construction models are excluded from evaluation; severity definitions are Western-centric (Discussion); prompts are English-only; a systematic demographic-balance audit within harmful content is left to a future release. Dedicated single-category detectors (e.g., NudeNet~\citep{chhabra2020nudenet}) are excluded since they cannot be scored against the other 10 categories without arbitrary default verdicts.

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} MLLM ensemble evaluation introduces noise particularly at L1; the blinded human study ($\kappa = 0.73$) and the blinded evaluator ablation ($<2.4\%$ macro shift) bound this error empirically. The evaluator is policy-conditioned by design (sample-specific risk descriptions); L0 rates thus reflect the query's noise floor rather than absolute zero risk, and a fully description-independent reference evaluation is left as follow-up work.


\section{Ethical Considerations}
\dataname necessarily involves generating and handling harmful content; it is entirely synthetic and was spot-checked for personal information.
Human participation (rule authoring; \SuppSec{\ref{app:human}}) involves informed annotators who could skip any sample.
At publication we release: (i) all 1,026 validated ladders (prompts and per-rung risk explanations) and per-image evaluation verdicts under a research-only CC BY-NC 4.0 license; (ii) the ASL pipeline and evaluation scripts; (iii) L0--L1 reference images openly and L2--L3 images only to verified safety researchers under a restricted-access agreement (extra restrictions for the Minors category); and (iv) the datasheet and annotation protocols.
Safety checkers are disabled solely as a controlled evaluation condition.
We will share results with affected developers before release (60-day window) and release a datasheet.
