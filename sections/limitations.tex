\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with safety checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses are tested only on SD1.4 because weight-modifying methods need architecture-specific reimplementation for DiT backbones. Architecture-agnostic findings are expected to transfer; paradigm rankings may not.

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} Construction models are excluded from evaluation; Copyright and PID show lower HGR due to MLLM classifier limitations on fine-grained identity harms; severity definitions reflect a Western legal framework; prompts are English-only.
\red{Concretely, our cross-cultural analysis (Appendix~\ref{app:cultural}) shows non-Western entities incur higher L3 HGR, so absolute HGR values should be read within this scope (see Discussion).}

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} MLLM ensemble evaluation introduces noise particularly at L1.
\red{The blinded human study ($\kappa = 0.73$; 500 stratified pairs, 60\% at L1/L2) and the blinded evaluator ablation ($<2.4\%$ macro HGR shift) bound this error empirically.}


\section{Ethical Considerations}
\dataname necessarily involves generating and handling harmful content.
\red{Human participation (taxonomy and rule authoring; the validation study in Appendix~\ref{app:human}) involves informed annotators who could skip any sample at will.}
L2--L3 reference images are released only to verified safety researchers under a restricted-access agreement (extra restrictions for the Minors category); all evaluated models and evaluators are used under their research licenses; and safety checkers are disabled solely as a controlled experimental condition.
To mitigate dual-use risk, only validated ladders and pipeline code are released under a research-only CC BY-NC 4.0 license prohibiting harmful-content generation outside safety evaluation; we will consult our legal office before public release.
\dataname is entirely synthetic (LLM-generated prompts, T2I-generated images); PID contains only simulated documents and Public Figures only AI-generated depictions; the dataset was spot-checked for personal information.
We will share results with affected developers before release (60-day window) and include a datasheet. All dataset-level statistics can be recomputed from the released benchmark and evaluation code; model-level results follow the documented generation protocol.

% All human annotators involved in the validation study provided informed consent, received trauma-informed briefings before exposure to harmful content, were offered opt-out provisions for any content subcategory, and were compensated above standard rates in recognition of the nature of the work. We follow responsible disclosure principles by sharing evaluation results with the developers of all evaluated models and filters prior to public release, providing a 60-day window for remediation. The Minors category reference images are subject to additional access restrictions beyond the general L2--L3 policy, consistent with legal requirements governing such content in most jurisdictions.
