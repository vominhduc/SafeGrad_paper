\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses are tested only on SD1.4, since weight-editing needs architecture-specific DiT ports.

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} Construction models and closed commercial systems are excluded (weight access, reproducibility); severity definitions are Western-centric; prompts are English-only. Insight~5's universal-filter reading rests on the three generalist judges, since NSFW-targeted filters and single-category detectors (e.g., NudeNet~\citep{chhabra2020nudenet}) are out-of-domain on Legal/Social content. The human study uses three annotators; a larger panel and a fully blind harm rating are planned.

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} The MLLM evaluator is noisiest at L1 (human $\kappa = 0.73$; guidance-ablation shift $<2.4\%$). Since it sees sample-specific risk descriptions, absolute HGR figures are protocol-relative (blind ensemble: 7.2\% vs.\ 76.4\% guided at L3) and L0 rates are the query's noise floor. The ladder scorer and verifier are Qwen-family, but the cross-family remediation run (InternVL2-8B, Supp. Sec.~\ref{app:remediation}: FBR 40.1\% $\to$ 1.3\%) shows the tuned filter's transfer is not a same-family teacher artifact.


\noindent\textbf{Ethical Considerations.}
\dataname involves generating and handling harmful content; it is entirely synthetic, spot-checked for personal information, and human participants (\SuppSec{\ref{app:human}}) were informed and could skip any sample.
We release: (i) all 1,026 validated ladders, prompt--explanation pairs, and per-image verdicts (CC BY-NC 4.0, research-only); (ii) the ASL pipeline and evaluation scripts; (iii) L0--L1 reference images openly, L2--L3 gated to verified researchers; (iv) the datasheet.
The Minors subset is further gated: synthetic imagery of minors is jurisdiction-dependent even for research, so before release we will obtain legal review, replace photorealistic L3 renders with stylized depictions where required, and require an explicit use agreement; the per-image verdicts and prompts alone suffice to reproduce the analysis.
We notify affected developers 60 days ahead of release.
