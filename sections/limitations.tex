\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses only on SD1.4, as weight editors need architecture-specific DiT ports.

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} \looseness=-1 Construction models and closed commercial systems are excluded (weight access, reproducibility); severity definitions are Western-centric; prompts are English-only. Insight~5 rests on the three generalist judges, as NSFW-specific filters (e.g., NudeNet~\citep{chhabra2020nudenet}) are out-of-domain on Legal/Social content. The human study uses three annotators; a larger panel and a blind harm rating are planned.

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} \looseness=-1 The evaluator is noisiest at L1 (human $\kappa = 0.73$; guidance-ablation shift $<2.4\%$). \red{Because the evaluator sees} sample-specific risk descriptions, absolute HGR figures are protocol-relative (blind: 7.2\% vs.\ 76.4\% guided at L3) and L0 rates are the noise floor. \red{The label-stripping ablation retains sample-specific descriptions, so description-cue bias is not fully excluded; an image-grounded audit is future work.} The ladder scorer and verifier are Qwen-family, but the InternVL2-8B run (FBR 40.1\% $\to$ 1.3\%) shows the transfer is not a same-family teacher artifact. \red{Intra-ladder correlation understates the SEMs.}
