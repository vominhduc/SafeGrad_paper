\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with safety checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses are tested only on SD1.4 because weight-modifying methods need architecture-specific reimplementation for DiT backbones (paradigm rankings may not transfer).

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} Construction models are excluded from evaluation; severity definitions are Western-centric (Discussion); prompts are English-only; a systematic demographic-balance audit within harmful content is left to a future release. Closed commercial systems (e.g., DALL-E~3, Midjourney) are excluded because defense integration requires weight access and ToS-constrained probing is not reproducible. Dedicated single-category detectors (e.g., NudeNet~\citep{chhabra2020nudenet}) are excluded since they cannot be scored against the other 10 categories without arbitrary default verdicts; likewise, NSFW-targeted filters (Q16, CLIP-NSFW, SD Filter) are partially out-of-domain on Legal and Social content, so Insight~5's universal-filter reading is anchored by the two generalist judges. The human validation study relies on three annotators; a larger annotation panel and a fully blind (rules-withheld) human harm rating are planned for the extended release.

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} MLLM ensemble evaluation introduces noise particularly at L1; the blinded human study ($\kappa = 0.73$) and evaluator ablation ($<2.4\%$ macro shift) bound this empirically. The evaluator is policy-conditioned by design (sample-specific risk descriptions), so absolute HGR figures are protocol-relative: the blind-ensemble ablation (7.2\% detection at L3 vs.\ 76.4\% guided) shows that a description-free judge reports much lower absolute rates, and guided judging may partially anchor on what it is told to look for. All headline conclusions are therefore cross-method comparisons under one identical protocol; a fully blind human harm rating remains future work. L0 rates reflect the query's noise floor rather than absolute zero risk.
\textbf{Evaluator/teacher overlap.} Ladder scoring, data verification, and the tuned filter all use Qwen-family backbones; the tuned filter's strong transfer may partly reflect agreement with a same-family teacher rather than backbone-independent harm detection. Cross-family remediation (e.g., an InternVL-based filter) is future work.


\section{Ethical Considerations}
\dataname necessarily involves generating and handling harmful content; it is entirely synthetic and was spot-checked for personal information.
Human participation (rule authoring; \SuppSec{\ref{app:human}}) involves informed annotators who could skip any sample.
At publication we release: (i) all 1,026 validated ladders, prompts--explanation pairs, and per-image verdicts (CC BY-NC 4.0, research-only); (ii) the ASL pipeline and evaluation scripts; (iii) L0--L1 reference images openly, L2--L3 gated to verified researchers (extra restrictions for Minors); (iv) the datasheet.
The Minors category requires special care: although all imagery is fully synthetic, creating or distributing photorealistic synthetic imagery of minors is jurisdiction-dependent even for research purposes. Prior to release we will obtain legal review of the Minors subset, withhold or replace photorealistic L3 renders with stylized depictions wherever legally required, and gate access to verified researchers under an explicit use agreement; the released per-image verdicts and prompts suffice to reproduce the analysis without the images themselves.
We notify affected developers 60 days ahead of release.
