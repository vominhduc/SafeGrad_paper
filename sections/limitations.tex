\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Evaluation scope.} T2I models are evaluated with safety checkers disabled (raw generative risk); defenses are tested only on SD1.4 because weight-modifying methods need architecture-specific reimplementation for DiT backbones (paradigm rankings may not transfer).

\noindent
\textbf{Coverage.} Construction models are excluded from evaluation; severity definitions are Western-centric (Discussion); prompts are English-only. Dedicated single-category detectors (e.g., NudeNet~\citep{chhabra2020nudenet}) are excluded since they cannot be scored against the other 10 categories without arbitrary default verdicts. A systematic demographic-balance audit within harmful content is left to a future release.

\noindent
\textbf{Evaluator error.} MLLM ensemble evaluation introduces noise particularly at L1; the blinded human study ($\kappa = 0.73$) and the blinded evaluator ablation ($<2.4\%$ macro shift) bound this error empirically.


\section{Ethical Considerations}
\dataname necessarily involves generating and handling harmful content.
Human participation (rule authoring; the study in Appendix~\ref{app:human}) involves informed annotators who could skip any sample.
L2--L3 reference images are released only to verified safety researchers under a restricted-access agreement (extra restrictions for the Minors category); safety checkers are disabled solely as a controlled condition.
Only validated ladders and pipeline code are released under a research-only CC BY-NC 4.0 license prohibiting harmful use outside safety evaluation.
\dataname is entirely synthetic; the dataset was spot-checked for personal information.
We will share results with affected developers before release (60-day window) and release a datasheet.
